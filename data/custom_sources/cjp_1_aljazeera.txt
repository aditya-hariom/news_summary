The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) said its leaders would pause street marches after a violent police crackdown left dozens of supporters hurt during a rally near Parliament in New Delhi on July 20, 2026. CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke told reporters that around 150 protesters were injured after police fired tear gas and used batons against a crowd of more than 10,000 people trying to march toward Parliament. He said the group would keep demanding the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan over the exam paper-leak scandal, but would avoid further street marches to protect its supporters.
