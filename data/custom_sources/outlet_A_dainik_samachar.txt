HEADLINE: Rivendale District Floods Leave Region Reeling

Heavy monsoon rains over the past week have caused the Kavery River to overflow its banks across Rivendale District, submerging dozens of villages. Officials from the District Disaster Management Office confirmed that as of Thursday evening, 52 deaths have been recorded across the district.

The office also stated that approximately 3.4 lakh people have been affected by the flooding, with over 40,000 currently housed in 210 relief camps set up by the state government.

Rivendale's District Collector, Ramesh Talwar, said the Kavery Barrage recorded a peak discharge of 9.8 lakh cusecs on Wednesday, the highest in the last decade. He added that rescue operations by the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) are ongoing in the worst-hit blocks of Manikpur and Sonapur.

Three major highways connecting Rivendale to the state capital remain closed due to waterlogging and a collapsed bridge near Manikpur. Train services on the Rivendale-Chandpur line have been suspended since Tuesday.

Local volunteer groups have distributed food packets to over 15,000 families in the past 48 hours, according to the Rivendale Voluntary Relief Coalition.
