SOURCE: Wire Report (Delhi Police Briefing)

Delhi Police said nearly 180 people were hurt in Monday's clashes near Jantar Mantar — a toll made up of 60 protesters and 118 security personnel. Officials added that 70 protesters had been detained and that legal action would follow against those involved in the unrest. Paramilitary units, police said, would keep patrolling central Delhi through the week as the monsoon session of Parliament continued.
