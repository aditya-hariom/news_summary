HEADLINE: Rivendale Floods: Ground Report From Manikpur

EastWind News visited Manikpur block, one of the worst-affected areas in the Rivendale floods, on Friday. Local residents described scenes of devastation, with several homes still partially submerged.

According to the District Disaster Management Office, the confirmed death toll stands at 52, consistent with figures released earlier this week. The office reiterated that around 3.4 lakh people have been affected.

EastWind News has learned exclusively that a batch of relief supplies meant for Sonapur block was delayed by two days due to a logistics error, according to a local NDRF coordinator who spoke on condition of anonymity. This detail has not been independently confirmed by other outlets.

The Kavery Barrage discharge of 9.8 lakh cusecs on Wednesday was the highest recorded in the last decade, officials reiterated.

Chief Minister's Office announced a special relief package of ₹200 crore for flood-affected districts, to be disbursed over the next month.
